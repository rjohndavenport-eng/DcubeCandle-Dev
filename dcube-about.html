Our Story

A light that continues to shine

A Light That Continues to Shine

DCube is more than a candle company. It's a family bonded by love, united in purpose, and inspired by the extraordinary light of our beloved Brianna.

Brianna was a beacon of joy. She had a gift for making people feel seen, valued, and loved exactly as they were. Her warmth was magnetic, her laughter infectious, and her kindness boundless. She approached life with a gentle strength and an unwavering belief in the good of others.

Brianna's symbol was the bee—a reminder of her tireless generosity, her ability to create something beautiful wherever she went, and the sweetness she brought to every life she touched.

When Brianna passed, our world dimmed. But even in our grief, we felt her presence—a gentle nudge to continue her mission of spreading love and light. So we created DCube as a living tribute to her spirit.

Every candle we make is hand-poured with intention and care. We use clean, natural soy wax and carefully chosen fragrances that evoke comfort, peace, and warmth. But what truly sets our candles apart is what you can't see: they're infused with the same love Brianna gave so freely.

The DeCubellis Family

Each flame is a continuation of her light, meant to illuminate spaces and uplift hearts. We believe that even the smallest acts—lighting a candle, taking a quiet moment, creating space for reflection—can transform a day. That's what Brianna taught us.

She showed us that love doesn't end; it transforms. It becomes something you carry forward, something you share, something that glows softly in the dark.

When you light a DCube candle, you're not just filling your home with fragrance. You're honoring a life lived with purpose. You're inviting warmth into your space. And you're joining us in keeping Brianna's light burning bright.

Thank you for being part of this journey. Thank you for letting us share her story with you. And thank you for bringing her light into your home.

With love,
The DeCubellis Family
