Lavender Dreams

Calming lavender with notes of vanilla and cedarwood. Hand-poured with natural soy wax, this candle brings peaceful tranquility to any space.

Handcrafted with love
Details ▾
  • 8 oz natural soy wax
  • 50 hour burn time
  • Cotton wick
  • Reusable glass vessel
  • Hand-poured in small batches
Care Instructions ▾

Trim wick to ¼" before each use. Allow wax to melt to edges on first burn (2-3 hours) to prevent tunneling. Never leave burning candle unattended. Keep away from drafts and flammable materials.
