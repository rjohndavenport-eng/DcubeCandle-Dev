Honoring Brianna's Light

DCube began as a way to celebrate the life and spirit of our beloved Brianna—a soul who radiated warmth, joy, and unconditional love. When we lost her, we channeled our grief into something luminous.

Each candle we create carries her light forward, crafted with care to bring peace and beauty into your space. Like the bee—Brianna's symbol—we work with purpose and devotion, creating something sweet from life's most tender moments.
