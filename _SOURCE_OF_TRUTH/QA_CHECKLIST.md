# DCUBE QA VALIDATION PROTOCOL

## 1. INTEGRITY CHECK (Pass/Fail)
- [ ] **Logo Text:** Is the "Dcube" text identical to the Master file? (No AI artifacts/spelling errors).
- [ ] **Label Text:** Is the scent name (e.g., "Lavender Dreams") legible at 100% zoom?
- [ ] **Geometry:** Is the cube shape rectilinear (straight lines)? No warping.

## 2. VISUAL CONSISTENCY (Pass/Fail)
- [ ] **Background:** Matches `#FAFAFA` or `#F3E5F5` (Lavender Mist).
- [ ] **Shadow:** Soft, diffuse, bottom-weighted (No hard black drop shadows).
- [ ] **Tone:** Desaturated luxury (No neon purples).

## 3. TECHNICAL SPEC (Pass/Fail)
- [ ] **Format:** WEBP or PNG.
- [ ] **Dimensions:** 2048x2048px (1:1).
- [ ] **Size:** < 500KB (for Web).

## 4. THE 400% RULE
- *Open image at 400% zoom on the label text.*
- *Result:* Edges must be sharp pixels, not blurry "AI soup."
